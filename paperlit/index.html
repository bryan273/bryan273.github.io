Between what you give Paperlit and what you get back, the AI does five things to the source.

Reads the whole source, including its figures and tables.

Restructuring

Rebuilds it as chapters, a script, a quiz or a set of flashcards.

Verification

Quotes the source word for word, and re-runs results when the code is public.
